### Build log, 2026-11-18 morning (9:30 pm to 9:15 am)

Every number is measured. Previous log: [evening of 2026-11-17](https://nanumos.com/log-2026-11-17-pm.html).

#### Visitors

At the time of writing, nanumos.com counts 252 people in the current window, 221 of them arriving directly. Robots are counted apart: 596 visits, not mixed in. I have not split this by device this morning; the table from the last log comes back next time.

#### Done (merged and checked)

- Try-out 0.1.9369 is live. It carries everything below in this list that touches the phone.
- Group chats: invite many people by link or QR code, leave, remove someone, set a group name and photo, mention people with @.
- Edit and unsend messages, sixteen emoticons we drew ourselves, banners that drop down from the top, and who is online.
- Group voice and video calls for up to four people.
- Calls ring even when the page is closed on phones that allow it (push notification), and the closer of two relay servers, Los Angeles or Seoul, is chosen automatically.
- A new Pager app: a green-screen pager on its own home tile with a beep, a voice message and number codes. You can page friends, and page Zeus, which answers back.
- The phone's first home page has a Google-look folder with Documents, Sheets, Slides, Drawings, Forms, Keep and To Do. All of them open in the try-out.
- Slides, Calendar and a single look for all Office apps are merged. A bug where Forms forgot the form you were working on, found while merging, is fixed.
- The news list grew from 309 to 360 sources, and 12 of them can be read in the browser (it was 7).
- The try-out now notes where people get stuck (counted without personal data), so I can fix those places first.
- Server backups: every six hours the site and the three call servers are copied to two other machines, and a restore into a fresh NanumOS is tried every day.
- The kernel health table was rebuilt: full test run, power-cut tests (about 2,900 and 1,650 states on the two chips, no lost data), a four-hour test of the desktop, and fuzzing. It found 4 open problems, all with fixes under way, below.

#### In progress (pushed, not merged)

- Disk: step 1 lets the save area grow to the size of the disk. Measured so far: files per save area 32,727 to 49,324 at 512 MiB, and 100,000 at 2 GiB, with power-cut tests showing no lost data. Some new red tests are still being compared with the old build.
- Desktop autosave pauses: the longest measured pause is 321 ms. A fix is being checked.
- Fuzz findings: memory blow-up in the git reader and a time-out in the mail reader, plus a test that wrongly said "ok". Fixes are being checked.
- Gmail-look mail app.
- The pager's drawing sometimes sits over the other windows on the live try-out. A fix is written; it needs its own second deploy today, which I have approved.
- One phone report: after a Safari reload, messenger conversations were gone. The WebKit tests on our side keep them, so I suspect storage failing silently. Being reproduced.
- A mobile assistant that works like one ordinary chat app, with an English wording pass.
- The first radio station follows the device's region.

#### Not done yet

- Leaving and coming back to Phone, Messenger and Pager does not restore the screen exactly (red test, same on the live build).
- The Windows try-out: apps do not open. Still unexplained.
- Mail sending comes later.
- Direct serving from our own server without the CDN is not switched on.

#### Next

1. Merge the pager fix and deploy a second try-out build today.
2. Land the disk growth and the kernel health fixes.
3. Find out why apps do not open on Windows.
4. Mobile Google-look apps: more depth in Documents, Sheets and Slides.
